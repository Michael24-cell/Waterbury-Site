What happens after I book?

+

We'll reach out within one business day to confirm a time and share a short intake form. No long onboarding. We come prepared and respect your time from the start.

Do I need to know exactly what I want before we talk?

+

Not at all. Most people come in with a vague sense that something isn't working. Part of what we do is help you figure out what the right move actually is. You don't need to have the answers — we'll find them together.

Can you improve my current site instead of rebuilding it?

+

Yes, and often that's the right call. Rebuilding from scratch makes sense in some situations, but targeted improvements can be faster and more cost-effective. We'll be honest with you about which approach fits your situation.

Do I have to commit after the first call?

+

No. The first visit is just a conversation. You'll leave with useful information about your site regardless of whether you hire us. There's no pressure and no follow-up pitch if it's not the right fit.

Can you show me a direction before I fully commit?

+

Yes. Before a full build engagement, we can show you a clear direction for the site — the visual structure, look, and strategy — so you know what you're getting before you fully commit.

How long does a typical project take?

+

It depends on the scope. An audit or focused improvement can take a week or two. A full custom build typically runs four to eight weeks. We'll give you a clear timeline before we start.

Do you offer ongoing work after the site launches?

+

Yes. We work with clients on retainer for ongoing optimization, content updates, performance monitoring, and continued improvements. A good website should keep getting better over time.

What does a project typically cost?

+

Most small business website projects start around $3,000 depending on scope, content, and functionality. Larger or more complex builds are scoped individually. Pricing is confirmed after the free consultation — not before — because the right approach depends on what we find.

Is there a reduced rate available for early projects?

+

Select projects may qualify for a reduced founding client rate after the consultation. This applies when the project has clear scope, ready assets, and is a strong fit for our current focus. It is not a general discount — it's offered intentionally for the right project at the right time. Book the free audit and we'll tell you honestly if your project qualifies.
